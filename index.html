Bio

From San Diego California, Sentinel was founded by Daniel Hernandez and Jes Verdugo in late 2007. Both combining melodic influences from classic bands like Iron Maiden, Black Sabbath, Judas Priest, to guitar oriented acts like Yngwie Malmsteen, Symphony X and more which can be heard in the self titled full length.

With a complete line up, Sentinel is ready to spread the shred throughout the land.
